# Trope lists — raw material for misremembering

Collected Oct 18, 2026 (conversation "Liminal spaces D&D campaign"). The design doc
(`the-back-room.md`) and the Corridor pool spec (`corridor-pool.md`) draw from these;
this file is the source, not the selection. Selection rule: the best tropes to
misremember are the ones players treat as *furniture* rather than events.

## Working list (ours), grouped by how they bend

**Objects that should be iconic but aren't quite right**
- Sword in the stone → the stone is there, the hilt is there, but what comes out is a spoon, a key, or a long bone. Or the stone is in the sword.
- The magic shop / "Ye Olde" item vendor → shelves of labeled potions, every label a word that is almost a word.
- The treasure chest at the end of the corridor → a mimic that doesn't attack, just breathes. Or it contains the party's own packs.
- The glowing-runes door that asks a riddle → the riddle has no answer because the door doesn't remember it; it just keeps asking.
- The map with "X marks the spot" → the X moves when nobody is looking at it.
- Torches in sconces that never burn out → lit, but the hallway behind you is dark again the moment you pass.

**Places that are always in-between**
- The tavern back room / cellar: stock, cots, a locked door to "the road." The road is just more cellar.
- The inn's stairwell; the hallway of identical guest rooms.
- The dungeon corridor, the 10-ft-wide passage with "a door at the far end."
- The stable, the market stall row at closing, the road at the crossroads, the ferry crossing.
- The campfire clearing where the party rests — the one place every TTRPG assumes is safe.

**People who are roles, not people**
- The mysterious hooded stranger in the corner booth, only ever seen from the booth.
- The wise old wizard mentor, mid-sentence forever; the sentence doesn't resolve.
- The barkeep who knows everything — about the *players*, not the characters.
- The farm boy / Chosen One, shoe-boxed in a cot, unsure which prophecy he belongs to.
- The quest-giver with an "exclamation mark" quality: repeats the hook verbatim.
- Guards who say "nothing to see here" about something very much there.

**Structural clichés the maze can quote**
- The prophecy — written on the wall, but it describes what the party did two rooms ago.
- The dark lord's tower on the horizon — visible from every window, never any closer.
- The three trials / three doors.
- The "you all wake up in a cell" cold open.
- The dragon's hoard — a nest-shaped depression in coins, warm, no dragon.
- The portal / mirror to another world — a reflection that misremembers the room behind it.

## Sourced top-10s (★ = good in-between material)

**Fantasy movies** — [WatchMojo, Top 10 Fantasy Movie Clichés](https://www.watchmojo.com/articles/top-10-fantasy-movie-cliches)
1. The Chosen One ★ (usually an orphaned farm boy)
2. The Prophecy ★
3. The Dark Lord
4. The Wise Old Wizard ★
5. Mythical Creatures (dragons, unicorns)
6. Magical Items ★
7. Different Races (elves, dwarves, goblins)
8. Magic Spells
9. Old English Accents
10. Haunted Forests ★
Honorable mentions: strange spellings and fictional languages; "darkness has fallen over the kingdom"; royal families; the epic final battle.

**Fantasy books** — [Fantasy-Faction, Ten Fantasy Clichés That Should Be Put To Rest](https://fantasy-faction.com/2013/ten-fantasy-cliches)
1. Prophecy or destiny
2. The orphan / chosen one, paired with the wise old wizard ("bearded deus ex machina")
3. The Dark Lord who is evil for its own sake
4. Good is beautiful, evil is ugly ★
5. Races are uniform (every elf is the same elf) ★
6. Men front and center; women on pedestals or martyred
7. Unrealistic fighting and wounds that heal overnight ★
8. Magic without limits
9. The witch-burning church
10. Strange spellings and excess apostrophes ★

**Fantasy MMOs** — [MassivelyOP, Perfect Ten: weirdly specific tropes in every fantasy MMORPG](https://massivelyop.com/2022/07/13/perfect-ten-weirdly-specific-tropes-found-in-every-fantasy-mmorpg/)
1. Giant spiders ★
2. Skimpy female armor
3. Cloth-wearing "glass cannon" mages
4. Only four elements
5. Death with no psychological cost ★
6. "Kill 10 rats" ★
7. Far more adventurers than farmers or merchants ★
8. Red health potions ★
9. Nobody eats, sleeps, or uses a bathroom ★
10. No children anywhere ★

**Console RPGs** — [The Grand List of Console Role Playing Game Clichés](https://www.talisman.org/~erlkonig/humour/rpg.html) (~190 entries; the ten best for us)
1. The Compulsories: there's always a fire dungeon, ice dungeon, sewer maze, misty forest, ghost ship ★
2. Buildings are bigger inside than outside ★
3. Every bookshelf holds one half-page book ★
4. A free night at the inn is never free — expect to be woken for a plot event ★
5. Two doors: the near one is locked, its key is behind the far one ★
6. Progress through a dungeon is marked only by the wallpaper changing ★
7. No switch is ever in the right position ★
8. Treasure is always behind the waterfall ★
9. The NPC who walks in circles and never arrives ★
10. Townsfolk repeat the same line no matter how many times you talk to them ★
